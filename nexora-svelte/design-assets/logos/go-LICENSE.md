# Go logo terms

Source: <https://go.dev/blog/go-brand> and <https://go.dev/brand>.

The Go graphic logo is a trademark of Google. The brand guidelines say:

> Only use the Go trademark in word mark form, i.e., plain text. Do not use the
> Go Logo or Go as a stylized form without permission.

Plain-text references to the Go language are allowed. Using the graphic logo in
a commercial product, modifying it, or placing it near another badge needs
review from `trademark@golang.org`.

**For this site:** the Go graphic logo is not published. The course card uses
the Go gopher instead, which has its own permission.

Files here:
- `go.svg` — official blue logo, downloaded from go.dev.
- `go-white.svg` — official white logo, for dark backgrounds.
- `go-gopher.svg` — the official gopher bust, from
  <https://go.dev/images/gophers/pilot-bust.svg>.
